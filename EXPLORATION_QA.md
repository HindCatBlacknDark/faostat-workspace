# Exploration verification

Completed 2026-11-19 against the local workspace.

## Automated checks

`node --check app.js`, `node --test tests/*.test.js`, and `python3 scripts/verify_export.py` passed. Seven test groups cover source integrity, unique coverage, all endpoint calculations, CSV escaping/round-trip, accent-insensitive search, every selected yearly value, original value text, and zero/missing/ambiguous observations.

## Browser interactions

- Searching `cote` finds Côte d'Ivoire. Selecting it increases the default comparison from three to four countries and the yearly table from 30 to 40 entries.
- Searching `zzzz` reports no matches while retaining all four selections and their 40 entries.
- Clearing and choosing Kenya, 2020–2022 produces six yearly entries, matching source values and units for both indicators.
- A seventh country cannot be selected after six are chosen. Six countries over three years produce 36 entries.

- Enter on Kenya's 2021 energy point displays 915 kcal/cap/d and E — Estimated value. Escape clears it. Pointer click at phone width opens the same detail panel.
- Default Algeria, Kenya and South Africa selection and 2019–2023 years restored after testing. Existing explanation draft text left intact.
